# Content sources
All quiz questions, case studies, datasets and the Round 3 scenario come from `Bizilytics_Battle_Replacement_Content_Pack` (installed under `content/`). The pack is fictional/illustrative business content aligned to the event rulebook; it carries no external source citations. The legacy Round 1 questions and their source links were retired (kept for rollback only in `legacy_backup/`).
Rubrics marked *proposed* in the organizer guides are NOT applied automatically; the app's configurable rubric weights (Controls) are unchanged.
